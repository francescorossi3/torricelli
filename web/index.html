Torricelli

Atmospheric parameters for the atmospheric correction of satellite data: water vapour, aerosols, profiles, air density, air and surface temperature, VNIR-SWIR (6S) and TIR radiative transfer

The name

Evangelista Torricelli (1608–1647), Galileo's pupil, invented the barometer (1643) and showed that air has weight: “we live submerged at the bottom of an ocean of air”. This project measures exactly that ocean of air between the satellite and the ground.

Torricelli is the atmospheric back end of a family of projects: Prognostica (weather forecast) and Columella (agriculture) read the radiative-transfer tables and atmospheric parameters from here; Torricelli reads the list of stations and calibration sites from Prognostica.

Data for other sites and programs

  • data/rt/lut_s2.json – 6S table for the Sentinel-2 bands (water vapour × AOD550 × solar zenith × altitude): T↓, T↑, T gas, path radiance, direct and diffuse E, spherical albedo
  • data/rt/lut_hyper.json – same table 400–2500 nm every 20 nm (PRISMA, EnMAP)
  • data/latest.json – last 24 h and 3-day forecast at every point
  • Full hourly archive (90+ columns, including 1000–200 hPa profiles and per-band parameters) as CSV: data/csv/atmos

Method

Surface and profiles: ECMWF IFS 0.25° (GFS fallback) via Open-Meteo; aerosols: CAMS. Air density ρ = p/(Rd·Tv). VNIR-SWIR: 6S code (Py6S), nadir view, continental aerosol, ozone 0.30 cm-atm. TIR Landsat 8/9 band 10: water-vapour functions of Jiménez-Muñoz et al. (2014). Updated automatically every day by GitHub Actions.
